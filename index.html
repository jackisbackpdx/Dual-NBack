GAMEPLAY

One game round presents 20+N flashing square's positions and letter sounds. So, for example if N = 1 (i.e. Dual 1-Back), you would be presented 21 square's position and letter sound pairs. After each round N is increased by one if there were less than three square's position match mistakes and less than three letter sound match mistakes. On the other hand N is decreased by one if there were more than five mistakes. In all other cases N remains the same for the next round.

There can be two kinds of mistakes: 1) not pressing audio (ear) or visual (eye) button when should have and 2) pressing audio or visual button when should not have.

SCORE

After every round the six counters show how the round went. The eye column counts square's position answers, the ear column counts letter sound answers.

    A column turns red when that sense collected three or more mistakes, which is what stops N from going up.

    TRAINING

    The ring counts the rounds you have finished today. Twenty rounds a day is the classic training protocol — that is about twenty minutes. The statistics screen plots your average N per day together with its trend.
